Locked thesis

EGM’s idea of rich, highly complete design documentation before construction can be taken further when those design docs are rich HTML — using the best visual and text tools for each aspect of the design (diagrams, mocks, sims, status, explainers).

Commonality with EGM: design-first, complete specs before build. Lived friction: markdown often bottlenecked AI→human communication of complex concepts. Tone: collaborative “yes, and” — not a format war, not judging EGM’s evidence.
