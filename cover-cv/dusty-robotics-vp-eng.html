Richard Bailey

Vice President of Engineering

SUMMARY
Vice President of Engineering, 16 years from Manager to VP, managing managers (leads and architects) across orgs of up to 70, and still a hands-on architect. Took hardware-enabled products to commercial launch (DAQRI Smart Helmet, Magic Leap, Kinect), with full-stack ownership from firmware to cloud, and cloud and AI-enabled systems. Throughline: registering digital models to the physical world — the core problem in construction layout.

EXPERIENCE
Quintar, Inc — Vice President of Engineering & Principle Engineer — Sep 2022 – Present
• Lead a 30-engineer international team across the USA and India; 100% retention over 2 years; set architecture, coding, and testing standards and defined staffing and training strategy ahead of Series B.
• Built a Python and ML stereo-video prototype (tracking, depth, segmentation) and moved it into production development for digital-twin sports viewing.
• Cut operations downtime 50% with operations monitoring; led OS, ARM and render-pipeline investigations that isolated memory corruption and threading hangs.

Niantic, Inc. — Senior Director, Lightship ARDK — Nov 2020 – Aug 2022
• Led a 15-engineer team to the on-time launch of Lightship ARDK at AWE, winning Best-in-Show AR; developer adoption rose 75%.

Magic Leap — Senior Director Software, Spatial AR Platform & Applications — Nov 2017 – Nov 2020
• Launched flagship Spatial AR Meetings within a year of prototype, with companion apps on PC, Mac, and iOS; designed device-to-cloud-to-device sharing APIs and delivered a 1000x application performance gain on the pre-V1 platform.

Margrave Technologies — Founder – CEO – Principle Developer, Jun 2016 – Nov 2017. VR Social Sharing SDK.

DAQRI — Director of Engineering and Chief Software Architect — Mar 2015 – Jun 2016

Microsoft — Manager & Architect, Automotive; Senior SDE, Kinect; Azure Storage — Apr 2008 – Mar 2015

Amazon.com — Senior Software Development Engineer, Mar 2007 – Apr 2008. Delivery time down 30%.

Cloud architecture under real failure

Task Improve the storage system, and build the image service.

Action He worked on Fast Multi-Paxos in Azure Storage and traced a memory corruption to a bit error after ruling out software causes, across a stated fleet of 500 million compute nodes. At Amazon he designed a low-latency image service and an 80TB migration to S3.

Result The Azure failure rate matched the expected bit-error MTBF. Amazon delivery time fell 30% for 900,000 books.

AI inside a product, not a side project

Result Kinect APIs shipped in Windows and Xbox. A more natural interaction cut test time 80%. The Quintar pipeline became a production effort for digital-twin viewing.
