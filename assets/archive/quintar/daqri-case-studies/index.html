Enterprise Engagements & Partnerships

  • Siemens

    DAQRI partnered with Siemens to evaluate AR for industrial maintenance, focusing on use cases like Yaw Motor Servicing, Wind Turbine Hub Inspection, and Gas Burner Assembly. DAQRI converted Siemens' PDF manuals into a 3D application ("Yaw Brake Service") that projected dynamic 3D animations directly onto physical yaw motors to guide engineers through maintenance steps.

  • Aprilia Racing (MotoGP)

    DAQRI collaborated with Realmore to develop a custom AR helmet for Aprilia's pit mechanics. This allowed them to visualize real-time bike telemetry, temperature data, and mechanical overlays directly in their field of view while working in the pit box.

  • Flex

    Partnered as a "Sketch-to-Scale" solutions provider to design, build, and deploy the smart glasses globally for enterprise workforces.

  • Autodesk

    Collaborated to route 3D CAD models directly into the DAQRI headset for construction and architectural verification.

  • Dell

    Integrated DAQRI into the Dell VR/AR Technology Partner Program to distribute the AR solutions for supply chain, maintenance, and field operations.

  • Oracle

    Integrated their IoT Applications with DAQRI hardware to display real-time IoT data workflows for asset management and situational awareness.

  • Plex (by Rockwell Automation)

    Deployed the headsets for demonstration and integration in manufacturing environments to boost productivity on the factory floor.

  • IBM & Emerson

    Partnered to integrate DAQRI's augmented reality overlays with their existing enterprise software, leveraging industrial workflow data.
